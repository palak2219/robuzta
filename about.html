About Robuzta

13+ Years of Repair Excellence

Our Journey

Founded in 2012, Robuzta Techlabs started with a simple mission: to provide fast, reliable, and affordable device repairs that people could trust.

Over the past 13+ years, we've grown from a small repair shop into a leading multi-brand device repair company serving thousands of happy customers across India. Our headquarters are in Ahmedabad, and we're expanding to serve more cities.

We specialize in chip-level laptop repairs, Apple device servicing, Microsoft Surface repair, and advanced data recovery. Our team of certified technicians uses only genuine parts and offers warranty on all repairs.
